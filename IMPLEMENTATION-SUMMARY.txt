WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================

A complete Free Item / Scheme Offer system for the ERP Billsprouts POS application that allows:

1. CREATE PROMOTIONAL SCHEMES
   - Admin → Schemes → Add Scheme
   - Configure: Name, product, minimum quantity, free quantity
   - Set dates, customer eligibility, sale type restrictions
   - Example: "Buy 10 Aspirin strips, Get 2 free"

2. AUTOMATIC SCHEME APPLICATION IN POS
   - When quantity changes, scheme eligibility checked automatically
   - Free quantity calculated: floor(qty / minimum) * free_qty (repeatable)
   - Displayed in cart with scheme name and free qty count
   - Works with existing billing workflow (no disruption)

3. SMART STOCK & GST HANDLING
   - Both paid and free quantities deducted from inventory
   - GST calculated only on paid quantities (not free)
   - Invoice shows paid and free separately
   - One-time stock deduction (no duplicates on retry)

4. FLEXIBLE CONFIGURATION
   - Customer type filtering (individual, retailer, wholesaler, distributor)
   - Sale type eligibility (retail, wholesale, distributor)
   - Non-repeatable schemes (one-time free qty)
   - Date range validation and active/inactive toggle

✅ AUTOMATIC SCHEME CHECKING

   - Non-repeatable schemes (one-time free qty)
   - Customer type restrictions
   - Sale type restrictions

✅ SMART CALCULATIONS
   - Free quantity: floor(quantity / minimum_quantity) * free_quantity
   - Example: Buy 10 Get 2 scheme
     - 5 qty → 0 free
     - 10 qty → 2 free
     - 20 qty → 4 free
     - 25 qty → 4 free (not 5, because 5 remainder < 10 minimum)

✅ PROPER BILLING
   - GST only on paid quantity (not free)
   - Invoice shows both paid and free
   - Stock deducted for total (paid + free)
   - No duplicate deduction on retry

✅ ZERO BREAKING CHANGES

   - Existing POS workflow unchanged
